Welcome to the WebSketch Extension Handbook — your guide to capturing web pages as structured grammar-based intermediate representation (IR) directly from Chrome.

## What is WebSketch Extension?

WebSketch Extension is a Chrome extension that captures the DOM of any web page and converts it into WebSketch IR — a grammar-based UI representation designed for LLMs. One click captures the page structure, styles, element bounds, and positioning, then copies the result to your clipboard.
