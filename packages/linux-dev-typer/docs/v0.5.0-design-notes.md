# v0.5.0 Design Notes — Continuity, Resilience, and Trust

## Core Principles

1. **Longevity over engagement.** The app does not chase daily active use. Returning after months is natural and welcome. No guilt, no penalty, no "streak" mechanics.

2. **Patience over urgency.** Systems adapt slowly. Suggestions surface only after high confidence. Comfort zones soften over time, not overnight. The app waits for the user, not the other way around.

3. **Stability over novelty.** Predictable behavior is a feature. Difficulty follows the weighting system without random spikes. Cues repeat rather than rotate. The completion card is calm.

4. **History compresses, never disappears.** When results exceed the 200-session cap, old sessions are summarized into monthly aggregates. The user's identity persists across schema migrations and version upgrades.

5. **Resilience to uneven progress.** Plateaus are normal — consistency is mastery. Short-term regressions don't trigger alarming language. A single bad session cannot destroy a comfort zone.

6. **User autonomy over system opinion.** Performance cues can be turned off. Personalization can be frozen or reset. Nothing is hidden or irreversible. The system responds, not predicts.

## Anti-Patterns (What We Reject)

- Urgency-driven engagement (streaks, daily goals, notifications)
- Predictive personalization (the app never says "you usually practice at...")
- Escalating judgment (no progression from "warning" to "fatigued")
- Red/green framing for performance (all signals use neutral tones)
- Optimization pressure (the app never implies the user should practice more)
