Envelope budgeting is a proven method where you allocate every dollar of income to a purpose before spending it.

## How it works

1. **Income arrives** — your paycheck, freelance payment, or any other source
2. **Allocate to envelopes** — distribute the full amount across named categories
3. **Spend from envelopes** — every transaction draws from a specific envelope
4. **Envelope hits zero** — stop spending in that category until next allocation

The key insight: you can only spend what is in each envelope. Overspending becomes structurally impossible.

## Ready-to-Assign

When income enters NextLedger, it lands in the Ready-to-Assign pool. This is unallocated money waiting for a job. Your goal each month is to bring Ready-to-Assign to zero by distributing every dollar to an envelope.

## Managing envelopes

- **Create** envelopes for every spending category you have
- **Group** envelopes by category (e.g., Housing, Transportation, Food) to keep the budget organized
- **Color** each envelope with a custom color for visual clarity
- **Allocate** monthly income across envelopes — every dollar gets a job
- **Move money** between envelopes when priorities shift
- **Hide** envelopes you do not need this month without deleting them
- **Archive** envelopes that are no longer active

## Savings goals

Set a goal amount and optional target date on any envelope. NextLedger tracks your progress toward the goal. You can also use auto-assign to automatically distribute Ready-to-Assign money toward envelopes with active goals.

## Monthly workflow

1. At the start of each month, allocate new income to envelopes
2. Log transactions as they happen (or import from CSV)
3. Review envelope balances before discretionary purchases
4. At month end, remaining balances carry forward to the next month via rollover

## Split transactions

Some purchases span multiple categories. NextLedger supports split transactions — a single purchase can draw from multiple envelopes with specified amounts. Each split line assigns a positive amount to a specific envelope.

## Budget periods

NextLedger organizes budgets into monthly periods. Each period tracks:

- **Total income** received that month
- **Total allocated** across all envelopes
- **Total spent** from all envelopes
- **Carried over** balance from the previous month
- **Ready-to-Assign** remaining unallocated funds

Periods can be closed to lock them from further changes, and recalculated if corrections are needed.
