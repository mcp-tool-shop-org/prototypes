# Privacy Policy

**Last Updated:** February 2, 2026
**Effective Date:** February 2, 2026

## Overview

Training Studio ("the App") is developed by MCP Tool Shop. This privacy policy explains how we handle information when you use our application.

## Data Collection

### What We Collect

**Training Studio does not collect, transmit, or store any personal data.**

The App operates entirely offline and locally on your device. Specifically:

- **No Analytics:** We do not use analytics services or tracking
- **No Telemetry:** We do not collect usage statistics or crash reports
- **No Network Requests:** The App makes no outbound network connections
- **No Account Required:** No registration or sign-in is needed

### What Stays on Your Device

All data processed by Training Studio remains exclusively on your local machine:

- ML training bundles you validate
- Validation results and reports
- Any configuration files you create

## Data Processing

Training Studio is a validation tool that:

1. **Reads** ML bundle files from your local filesystem
2. **Validates** bundle structure, manifests, and file hashes
3. **Outputs** validation results to your console or local files

No data leaves your device during this process.

## Third-Party Services

Training Studio does not integrate with any third-party services that collect data:

- No cloud storage integration
- No external APIs called
- No advertising networks
- No social media integrations

## Open Source Dependencies

The App uses the following open-source libraries:

| Library | Purpose | Privacy Impact |
|---------|---------|----------------|
| TensorFlow.js | ML model parsing | None - local only |
| Chart.js | Visualization | None - local only |

These libraries operate entirely within the App and make no network requests.

## Children's Privacy

Training Studio does not collect any information from anyone, including children under 13 years of age.

## Data Security

Since Training Studio:
- Collects no data
- Makes no network requests
- Stores nothing remotely

There is no transmitted data to secure. Your local files are protected by your operating system's security mechanisms.

## Your Rights

Under GDPR, CCPA, and similar regulations, you have rights regarding your personal data. Since Training Studio collects no personal data, these rights are inherently satisfied:

- **Right to Access:** No data to access
- **Right to Deletion:** No data to delete
- **Right to Portability:** No data to export
- **Right to Opt-Out:** Nothing to opt out of
