Welcome to the Dev-Op-Typer Handbook. This is the complete guide to getting the most out of your typing practice.

## What is Dev-Op-Typer?

Dev-Op-Typer is a typing practice app built specifically for developers. Instead of typing random words or prose, you type real code — Python, JavaScript, C#, Java, SQL, and Bash — with every symbol, brace, and semicolon counting toward your score.

The app watches how you perform and adapts: when you are comfortable, it challenges you with harder snippets. When you are struggling, it eases back. All of this happens locally on your machine with no accounts, no telemetry, and no cloud sync.
